Highlight shortcut (#4085)

On Windows/Linux, Ctrl+Alt+H toggles the last chosen highlighter colour (yellow in a new session). If every character in the formatting target already has that colour, it applies explicit No Color; otherwise it applies the chosen colour to the target. Choosing another colour applies it, and choosing No Color makes that the current choice. Explicit format.highlight {"color": ...} remains an idempotent setter; omitting color uses the toggle. Ribbon and mini-toolbar colour buttons remain setters, not toggles. The highlight decision checks the complete target, including mixed colours after 10,000 runs; formatting-state queries retain their existing sampling limit. Fully selected paragraph marks (including whole cells and empty paragraphs) participate in that decision; partial text columns ignore marks outside the selected range. Palette state is outside document undo; the formatting itself is undoable. The existing selection/word/pending-caret targeting is unchanged (the separate request #4086 is not addressed).

Regressions: highlight_shortcut_toggles_the_last_colour and highlight_toggle_handles_mixed_inherited_and_pending_formatting. Synthetic command/key qualification, with additional large-selection, non-mutating preview, whole-cell mark, empty-paragraph mark and partial-column controls. Native Word comparison has not been performed for this change.

Sample text (#1067)

Enter at the end of a paragraph containing exactly =rand() or =lorem() replaces the formula with five paragraphs of three sentences and leaves the caret in a following empty paragraph. =rand(p) / =lorem(p) set the paragraph count; (p,s) sets both counts. WordCraft accepts integers from 1 to 200 for each count; if either parameter is invalid or outside that range, both revert to 5/3. Other text, a selection or Enter inside the formula follows ordinary Enter behaviour. The English text is original to WordCraft; lorem ipsum uses traditional pseudo-Latin. This implements the issue's requested defaults; the exact numerical limits have not been compared in Word.

The replacement and its paragraph breaks are one undo step. Typing and paragraph formatting use the existing editing primitives. Lists

Bullets and Numbering decide whether to toggle off from the selected paragraphs' effective numbering, including numbering inherited from a paragraph style or its basedOn chain. An explicit numId=0 override remains unlisted until a list is applied. The effective list level and any level override determine the list kind, including an explicitly requested gallery kind. Toggling preserves custom styles, including a ListParagraph style that supplies numbering, list definitions and paragraphs outside the selection. A directly applied list still clears the automatic ListParagraph style. Original command and DOCX round-trip tests cover this narrow case related to #3459; it is not a new Word UI measurement. AutoCorrect and AutoFormat as you type

The --> replacement produces → when completed with a space (#2223), including at paragraph start without creating a list. It respects AutoCorrect preferences, user entries and undo. The existing -> paragraph-start arrow bullet is unchanged.

Applied when a word is finished by a space, punctuation or Enter (enter_finishes_a_word_for_autocorrect). The word starts after the last white-space character before it, also when that is a no-break space or an ideographic space (autocorrect_finds_the_word_after_a_wide_space):

  • First letter of a sentence (paragraph or list item start, after . ! ?) is capitalised; not after abbreviations (e.g., Dr., …) or initials (J.), and not for mixed-case words (iPhone) or single-letter labels (a.). teh→the, i→I, (c)→©, (tm)→™, wait...→wait…, smart quotes (autocorrect_as_you_type); a quote opens after a space, an opening bracket, a dash, « ‹ „ ‚, or the other kind of opening quote, so nested quotes curl correctly (smart_quotes_nested_and_opening_delimiters). Smart quotes in Hebrew (and other right-to-left text) are chosen from the characters around the quote in logical order, whatever the display direction: " after a space or at the start is “ (U+201C) even though it shows on the right of the word, after a letter ” (U+201D); in an acronym (צה"ל) it becomes ” as in Word for Mac 16.113.4, not gershayim (״); ' after a letter (ג'ירפה) is ’ as after any letter, with no geresh conversion (not yet checked in Word; smart_quotes_in_hebrew_use_logical_order, #1670).

Any other Undo (of later typing, of a paste) adds nothing, and the capital is forgotten once another Undo runs, a new document is opened, or the undo history is cleared. The first word of a paragraph has no word before it, so nothing is added there; Word also left the retyped first word ("hello") lowercase in that paragraph, which WordCraft doesn't (paragraph-level suppression, only for the paragraph where the Undo happened: Word capitalises the first word of a new paragraph again; a separate follow-up). Spelling as you type (#1552)

Word draws no red squiggle under the word being typed. The squiggle comes when the word ends (space, punctuation, Enter, Tab) or when the caret leaves it any other way (arrow keys, Home, End, a click, a selection). F7 and the Spelling pane report the word all the while. Grammar marks are not held back.

Text direction

  • Typing Arabic, Hebrew or another right-to-left script as the first letters of a paragraph whose direction nothing sets (not the paragraph, its style or the document defaults) makes it a right-to-left paragraph, so the caret starts at the right margin. A paragraph that already has letters keeps its direction, and so do opened documents: a paragraph without w:bidi stays left-to-right whatever its text (typing_rtl_letters_into_an_empty_paragraph_makes_it_rtl). Word takes the direction from the keyboard language instead; not yet compared side by side.
  • A table inserted in a right-to-left paragraph is a right-to-left table (its first cell at the right), as in Word, so Tab moves to the cell on the left (the next one in reading order) and Shift+Tab back; in a left-to-right table Tab still moves right (#2634, tab_in_a_table_inserted_in_right_to_left_text_moves_left).
  • Ctrl+Tab (⌥Tab on macOS) types a tab character in a table cell instead of moving to the next cell (#3137, ctrl_tab_inserts_a_tab_inside_a_table_cell).
  • Text typed or inserted (links, fields, symbols, pasted text with merged formatting) takes its direction from its own letters, not from the run around the caret, keeping only that run's bidi language: Latin typed into Hebrew from a reopened file is saved as left-to-right text, and the saved file marks the right-to-left pieces w:rtl itself (#1543, latin_typed_into_reopened_hebrew_is_saved_left_to_right). Known display difference: spaces, digits and punctuation typed into reopened Hebrew text draw in the Latin font, not the complex-script font, until the file is saved and reopened.

Layout

  • No line break right after a slash (and/or, URLs) unless the word can't fit a line (layout::tests::no_line_break_right_after_a_slash). A slash that opens a word after a space (Kazakh /Russian captions) travels with it: the break is before the slash, so /заполняется moves to the next line whole and is never split between its letters (#2963, layout::tests::a_word_after_a_slash_wraps_whole, also in a fixed table cell and not after a no-break space).

Complex scripts

  • Myanmar, Khmer and the Indic scripts are typed in memory order (consonant, medials, then vowel signs such as ေ, which is drawn before its consonant); the caret steps over whole syllables (Myanmar vowel signs and stacked consonants never split off), Backspace removes one character (vowel sign, medial, stacked consonant), Delete the whole syllable — the platforms' standard behaviour, not yet compared against Word (#604, myanmar_typing_steps_by_syllable_and_backspaces_by_sign, indic_and_khmer_backspace_removes_one_character).

Input methods

  • An input method can ask to erase characters before and after the caret (DeleteSurrounding) and send replacement text. The web build's hidden text field does this whenever its contents change other than by adding to the end: Backspace and corrections from phone keyboards, and the Chrome caret jump below. The 0.37 desktop bridge can also deliver character- or byte-counted surrounding deletions from the input method. WordCraft erases those characters as Backspace erases a selection, stopping at the paragraph's ends, then types the new text. The new text keeps the erased text's formatting and never overtypes what follows. Erasing and retyping stay in the open typing undo step, and typing after them joins it too (#1480, keys::tests::delete_surrounding_from_the_web_text_agent_does_not_repeat_text, delete_surrounding_erases_characters_on_both_sides_of_the_caret, delete_surrounding_stays_inside_the_paragraph, delete_surrounding_joins_the_typing_undo_step, delete_surrounding_keeps_the_replaced_texts_formatting, delete_surrounding_text_does_not_overtype, delete_surrounding_rewrite_ends_at_a_key_press).

  • After AutoCorrect or AutoFormat changes the text as you type, the web build tells its hidden text field to start afresh. Its later counts then can't reach past the change (automatic_change_asks_the_input_method_to_start_afresh). The reset itself happens in eframe and hasn't been checked in a browser.

  • On the desktop, a text box that keeps the keyboard while its dialog is open (Search, Go To's page box, the Icons search, a new caption label) asks for focus only while it lacks it. egui resets the input method on every focus request, and on X11 a reset recreates the XIM input context, so asking every frame reset it every frame and could leave typing dead until a restart (#2986, dialogs::tests::boxes_that_keep_focus_do_not_interrupt_ime_every_frame).

  • While an input method composes (Japanese, Chinese…), its marked text is drawn from the caret, and the caret and the candidate window sit at the input method's cursor inside it: the end of the range it reports, or the end of the marked text when it reports none. The range is clamped to the text (canvas::tests::the_caret_follows_the_input_method_cursor_in_the_composition).

  • A composition belongs to the text it was started on. When a command replaces the body under the same document id — Replace Document Text, a version restore, Compare, Undo/Redo — or opens a new document, the body's replacement epoch advances, the platform clears the composition, and any marked text is dropped. Replacing the text with an identical one still starts a new context (canvas::tests::ime_generation_changes_on_body_replacement_with_the_same_text, ime_generation_changes_on_undo_and_version_restore, keys::tests::observe_ime_drops_a_preedit_without_focus). Undo/Redo also gives the equation being edited a fresh session token, so its context follows the restored tree. The marked text is never painted without focus.

  • If replacing the body or changing the edited equation invalidates nonempty marked text, the next canvas input batch rejects that composition's queued surrounding deletions and one replacement commit before any engine mutation. An associated Text fallback is rejected too. A fresh nonempty preedit or ordinary typing ends that batch's suppression; without an old marked composition, direct commits remain eligible. A later commit after the rejected one, or in a later canvas batch, is also fresh input. New regression checks cover identical-body replacement, both deletion units, equation exit before or within the batch, unchanged text and Undo history, and fresh input (keys::tests::ime_interrupted_body_preedit_drops_queued_replacement_before_mutation, ime_interrupted_equation_preedit_drops_queued_body_replacement, ime_interrupted_preedit_does_not_suppress_fresh_input). Their managed validation is pending; this does not claim native input-method acceptance or identify untagged events across frames.

  • An accepted IME commit earns an InputMethod receipt only when the engine inserted exactly the committed text. A single quote the engine leaves as typed (smart quotes off) is the input method's own change; a smart quote or AutoCorrect replacement is Other (ime_apostrophe_with_smart_quotes_off_is_an_input_method_change, ime_apostrophe_with_smart_quotes_on_stays_other, ime_literal_commit_after_autoformat_is_input_method). A DeleteSurrounding whose two sides cannot both be applied is rolled back whole and publishes no receipt (a_refused_second_delete_surrounding_rolls_the_request_back); every such refusal also interrupts the composition and clears the painted pre-edit and both replacement joins because the platform's hidden text already changed (a_refused_body_delete_clears_preedit_and_joins). Under Track Changes, deleting text this author inserted shortens the stored paragraph, so the following range and caret are shifted by the bytes actually removed (tracked_delete_surrounding_shifts_past_its_own_insertion, tracked_own_insertion_then_a_refused_side_rolls_the_whole_request_back). Emptying a content control restores its placeholder, which may make the paragraph longer; a body deletion maps the following range and caret by the signed stored-length change, and refuses the whole transaction when growth cannot be proven to come from a placeholder restored at or before the erased range (delete_surrounding_maps_after_text_and_caret_past_a_growing_placeholder, delete_surrounding_maps_a_shrinking_placeholder_and_after_text, delete_surrounding_maps_a_placeholder_restored_by_the_after_side). Control markers are zero-length visible objects, so a range start maps after the start marker and after the end marker: an ordinary IME deletion never breaks a control (ime_text::tests::control_markers_are_not_included_in_delete_ranges). The replacement's undo join is scoped to the next text.insert at the selection the deletion left; an intervening command owns its own step (body_delete_join_does_not_leak_to_an_intervening_command).

  • Inside an equation, DeleteSurrounding erases the counted characters with the equation's own transactional command (at most 64 units a side), refusing the whole request if any step cannot delete — including a step that would only move the caret into another argument (equation_delete_surrounding_refuses_a_mixed_caret_only_step); the delete and the following commit are one undo group. Byte counts have no reversible mapping into the structured tree, so the whole request is refused, the composition is interrupted and the replacement the platform sends for that rejected deletion — the immediately adjacent Commit, or an adjacent Text + Commit pair — is dropped instead of being inserted beside the untouched equation (the pre-edit never lives in the model). Any later Text/Commit in the same raw-input batch is fresh input and applies, as is a later direct commit without a pre-edit; no persistent tombstone can swallow it. An over-cap request clears the painted pre-edit and any earlier deletion's replacement join before refusing. The equation's surroundings stay explicitly unavailable (ime_equation_delete_is_one_input_method_change, ime_equation_delete_and_commit_are_one_undo_step, ime_equation_byte_delete_drops_only_its_immediate_replacement, over_cap_equation_delete_clears_the_painted_preedit, over_cap_equation_delete_clears_the_previous_replacement_join). The engine's checked revision on the unavailable stamp proves a successful content change; a refusal, an automatic rewrite or an exhausted revision publishes no receipt (ime_transformed_equation_commits_are_other, exhausted_ime_revisions_never_certify_equation_or_wide_body_edits).

  • Pre-edit and commit sizes are bounded (the visible window at 4000 bytes, a pre-edit at the same cap, a commit at 64 KiB): larger hostile events are ignored rather than painted or inserted (oversized_ime_events_are_ignored).

Known gaps

  • Web (#1480): Chrome has been seen moving the caret of eframe's hidden text field to its start after the first non-Latin character. Later letters then go in before what was typed since the field was last cleared, so hello then мир gives риhelloм. The text is no longer repeated, but the cause of the caret jump hasn't been found and isn't worked around.
  • Word's body font Aptos isn't installed outside Office; our substitute (Carlito, else Helvetica Neue) is wider, so lines wrap earlier than in Word. Needs a metric-matched open substitute.
  • Moving paragraphs with Track Changes on isn't recorded as a tracked move; whole table rows don't move yet (a selection across cells is refused). Go Back's places aren't mapped through later edits (they are clamped to the document).
  • Not yet compared: keystroke-by-keystroke comparison of Correct TWo INitial CApitals, cAPS LOCK and table-cell capitals (implemented from Word's AutoCorrect options; Word's exception list not compared); the rows marked ¹ above.

  • WordCraft: the headless ui_shot example (no window, no focus) with UI_SHOT_FULL=1 prints the whole document.inspect; drive it with ui.text / ui.key lines so keys take the real input path.
